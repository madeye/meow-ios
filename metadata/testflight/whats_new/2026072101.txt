Build 2026072101 (1.4.0)

• DNS answers now carry the real upstream TTL instead of a fixed 60-second value, so apps re-resolve on the server's schedule.
• The IP-to-hostname DNS table is saved to disk and reloaded when the engine starts, keeping domain-based routing rules working for connections opened before a restart.
• Removed all in-place engine restarts (wake, network change, address-family change) — restarts caused multi-second DNS blackouts; connections now recover on their own per flow.
• Please test: lock/unlock cycles, Wi-Fi↔cellular switches, and long idle periods. The VPN should stay connected and traffic should recover without toggling it.
