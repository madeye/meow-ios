• Share servers as QR codes: Shadowsocks servers export as standard ss:// links, URL subscriptions export their subscription URL — scan with any compatible client
• Test a whole proxy group with one tap; each proxy row now shows its protocol type
• Built-in YAML editor for any subscription (pencil icon next to the refresh button)

Known limitation: non-DNS UDP traffic is not yet forwarded by the tunnel — QUIC/HTTP3 falls back to TCP HTTP/2 (usually transparent to the user). Fix is in progress.
